## Self-contained local app

The app runs with `node server.js` and requires no cloud account, API key, Supabase project, payment service or printer. Its HTML/CSS/JavaScript runtime has no third-party runtime dependency. Local username/password registration and PIN controls are available; online identity, email verification/recovery and cloud sync remain future work. Speech recognition is optional and requires consent; typing works without it. No money is sent by recording a sale, purchase or refund.
